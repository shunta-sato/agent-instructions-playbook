# Japanese technical writing

Match the requested audience and existing document conventions. Define unfamiliar
terms at first use, keep identifiers exact, and prefer natural Japanese sentences
over unexplained English noun chains. Do not translate commands, API names or
error messages in a way that makes them unusable.

Separate confirmed behavior, assumptions, inference and unverified claims. Preserve
numbers, units, conditions and source attribution. Lead with the reader's decision
or usable result, then explain the necessary mechanism and limits.

Use a table only for genuine comparison and headings for navigation. Do not impose
a fixed report outline, an extra summary or a glossary on every small edit. Remove
redundant narration without deleting non-obvious constraints or useful examples.
