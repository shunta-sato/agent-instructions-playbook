# Research synthesis

Separate supported, falsified, still-open and exploratory-only findings. Compare
against the same workload and evaluation protocol; retain rejected alternatives and
negative results. Do not overstate a pilot, a selected best run or a synthetic load.

Choose continue, pivot, stop or productization from the evidence and remaining value.
No fixed number of results forces synthesis, and no mandatory report pack is needed.
Keep the decision and evidence references in the existing research record.

For productization, retain behavior/interaction/quality/API contracts, accepted evidence
and limits, unresolved obligations and the disposition of prototype code. Rebuild from
the confirmed contract by default; exploration code is not authoritative production
source. Reuse validated data or non-runtime assets when appropriate and authorized.

Production implementation must satisfy its actual required conditions and checks.
The result cannot inherit release readiness from a successful experiment. When a
project uses the bundled research promotion checker, its explicit evidence format
is documented in `references/promotion.md`; this is an opt-in machine contract, not
a universal workflow or a requirement to load a retired delivery skill.
